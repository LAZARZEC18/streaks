        Fast to tick off, honest about streaks, and clear about what to do next.

        Questions

        How are streaks counted?
        A daily streak is the run of consecutive days you completed, counting back from today. If today isn't done yet, the streak through yesterday stays alive until midnight. Count habits only count a day once you reach the goal. Weekly habits count weeks, Monday to Sunday, where you hit your target.
        Why is a weekly habit sometimes left out of today's ring?
        A habit like "Gym, 3 times a week" isn't due every day. It joins today's ring when you do it, or when you need every remaining day of the week to reach the goal. Otherwise it shows as optional.
        Do reminders work when the tab is closed?
        No. Reminders work while Streaks is open in a browser tab, because a web page can't wake itself up. Keep it pinned in a tab and you'll get a notification at the time you set, or an in-page alert if notifications are off.
        Where is my data stored?
        Only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data removes it, so export a backup now and then.
        How do I move to a new phone or laptop?
        Export a backup here, open Streaks on the other device and import the file. Every habit, check-in and note comes across.
        I forgot to tick yesterday. Can I fix it?
        Yes. Open the habit and select the day on its calendar, or pick the day on the year grid. Streaks update straight away.
